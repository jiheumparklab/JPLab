AI for Early Cancer Detection and Precision Medicine

About the Lab

JPLab develops computational methods to advance early disease detection, personalized screening, and precision medicine. Our research uses longitudinal electronic health records (EHRs), genomic data, and modern AI methods to model patient trajectories and support clinically meaningful decisions.

We work with clinical and computational collaborators across healthcare institutions to develop methods that are robust, interpretable, and relevant to real-world care. We welcome research collaborations and inquiries from prospective trainees.

Research

AI for Early Cancer Detection

We develop EHR-based predictive models and foundation models to identify patients at elevated cancer risk, study transferable signals across cancer types, and support personalized screening and risk stratification across healthcare systems.

Interpretable and Trustworthy Clinical AI

We investigate knowledge-augmented AI, longitudinal patient representations, and clinician-facing interfaces to make predictions more interpretable, evidence-grounded, and useful in clinical decision-making.

Precision Medicine and Treatment Optimization

We combine EHR and genomic information to improve risk assessment and study treatment effectiveness and safety. Our interests include genetic risk prediction, real-world evidence, and causal inference for individualized treatment decisions.
